MirrorDesk for macOS (tester build)
==================================

Mirror an iPhone, iPad or Android phone to a browser on this Mac, with sound.
Needs: Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, or an Intel Mac with macOS 12 or newer.

1. Install: open Terminal (Applications > Utilities) and run
       sh ~/Downloads/MirrorDesk/install.sh
   (or double-click "Install MirrorDesk.command"; INSTALL-GUIDE.html explains what to do
   when macOS refuses to open it). It takes 10-30 minutes the first time.
2. Read TESTER-GUIDE.html: how to connect each phone, what to try, how to report problems.

Afterwards open MirrorDesk from the Applications folder in your home folder (or Spotlight).
To remove it: double-click "Uninstall MirrorDesk.command" (or: sh install.sh --uninstall).
